## Ian Chen

I explore reliable tool-using AI agents through evaluation, rubric quality,
trajectory-level diagnosis and controlled improvement experiments.

### Research Interests

- **LLM Agent Evaluation** — trustworthy measurement of realistic agent behavior
- **Rubric Quality** — whether grading criteria measure the intended behavior
- **Reward Modeling** — the relationship between evaluation and useful learning signals
- **Reinforcement Learning** — training-signal quality and generalization

### Current Work

[Agent Improvement Lab](../projects/agent-improvement-lab/) is an independent
public research project. Its first milestone is the design of Evaluation
Harness v0.1 for tau2 Retail.
